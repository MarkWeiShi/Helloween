# Play unlock screen

The initial scene renders the actual rotating pumpkin map under an inert app shell. Only the lock icon and Enable Mic & Play control are interactive. Old welcome artwork, intro copy, sound switch and skip action are removed. Valid invitation routes show the gameplay and microphone confirmation dialog immediately on arrival, without first requiring Enable Mic & Play. Confirming requests microphone permission and then opens and plays the invited character transformation directly. Dismissing the dialog leaves the world locked; Enable Mic & Play can reopen it. There is no second Accept invitation step after unlock. Expired invitations retain the disabled invitation notice.

After tapping unlock, the user confirms the gameplay and microphone notice. For invitations, this notice explains the two-second recording and automatic upload for the inviter alone to play. The confirmation click resumes a persistent Web Audio context directly in the gesture and requests browser microphone permission. Successful permission stops the temporary input tracks immediately, enables game sound, animates the lock shackle and button for 700 ms, plays a quiet ascending chime, then removes the input shield. The permission check itself does not measure or upload audio. Once unlocked, the invited transformation uses the consent just given; its existing recording, cancellation, retry and replay protections apply. Denial/unavailability leaves the shell inert and shows a retry message. Reduced motion uses a short transition without scaling. Browser permission cannot be bypassed.

The existing unlock suite covers the inert state, simulated microphone/audio unlock and refusal/retry. Real device permissions, speaker output and embedded browser restrictions require device acceptance.

Regression coverage: `tests/browser/invite-unified.spec.ts` checks immediate consent on arrival, no microphone request before confirmation, direct playback after confirmation, dismissed consent, permission denial and expired links. `tests/browser/recording.spec.ts` checks private upload, local-only exploration, cancellation, retry and no duplicate upload on replay. Tests use synthetic microphone input.
