用户数据删除说明

Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

删除收到的录音

在游戏中打开“朋友尖叫”，找到录音，点击“删除声音”并确认。此操作删除该条服务器录音及其关联录音详情；它不等于删除账号、邀请或全部互动记录。若你是录音者，可申请删除自己上传的录音。

申请删除自己的数据

申请时请注明“Halloween Tricks 数据删除”，说明希望删除整个游戏账号还是指定录音，并提供使用的登录方式、游戏昵称，以及可帮助定位记录的邀请链接或大致使用时间。不要提供密码、验证码、Cookie、访问令牌或身份证件。

我们会核验你对账号或录音的控制权，避免他人冒名删除。整账号申请包括可关联到你的游戏资料、进度、邀请、互动、上传录音及本应用的登录关联；也可要求核查身份服务中的关联数据。处理结果及需要补充的信息会通过申请渠道回复。本游戏不会替你删除 Facebook、WhatsApp 或其他 SNS 账号。

浏览器数据、授权与备份

可通过浏览器的网站设置清除 helloween.fly.dev 的 Cookie 和本地存储。游客请先保留用于核验的信息，否则清除标识后可能无法定位账号。清除浏览器数据、退出登录或在 Facebook“应用和网站”设置中移除本应用，仅影响本机或授权，不代表服务器数据已删除。

删除申请处理时，会说明相关备份的清理或隔离安排；因法律要求或安全事项必须保留的有限记录，也会说明范围和理由。已无法关联个人的汇总统计，以及其他用户自行下载、复制或在第三方平台转发的内容，可能无法通过本游戏删除。

联系我们

隐私、数据查询、更正或删除申请，请发送邮件至 support@hellotalk.com。请在标题中注明 Halloween Tricks。我们将通过该邮箱回复并核验申请。

Data Deletion Instructions

Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk · Updated October 31, 2026

Delete a received recording

Open “Friends’ screams”, find a recording, choose “Delete voice” and confirm. This removes that server recording and its associated recording details, not the account, invitation or all interaction records. If you recorded the audio, you may request deletion of your own uploaded recording.

Request deletion of your data

Use the subject “Halloween Tricks data deletion”. Specify whether you want to delete your game account or particular recordings. Include your sign-in method, game nickname, and an invitation link or approximate usage date to help locate the records. Never send passwords, verification codes, cookies, access tokens or identity documents.

We verify control of the account or recording to prevent unauthorized deletion. An account request covers game profile, progress, invitations, interactions, uploaded audio and application login associations linked to you; you can also request review of associated identity-service data. We reply through your request channel with results or any information needed. We do not delete your Facebook, WhatsApp or other SNS accounts.

Browser data, authorization and backups

You can clear cookies and local storage for helloween.fly.dev in browser settings. Guests should retain identifying information first: losing the browser identifier may prevent us from locating their records. Clearing browser data, signing out, or removing this app in Facebook's “Apps and websites” settings does not itself delete server data.

When handling your request, we explain relevant backup removal or isolation arrangements and the scope and reasons for any limited legal/security retention. Statistics that can no longer be linked to individuals and copies downloaded or forwarded by others may not be removable through this game.

Contact

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, email support@hellotalk.com and include Halloween Tricks in the subject. We reply and verify requests through this address.
