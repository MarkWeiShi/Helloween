隐私政策

Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

本政策适用于 helloween.fly.dev 上的 Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk 网页游戏，说明本游戏如何处理你的数据。

我们处理哪些数据，以及为什么

  • 账号与游戏记录：浏览器访客标识、登录服务提供的账号标识、你填写的昵称和国家／地区、游戏进度、分数、邀请链接与互动记录，用于识别账号、保存游戏、归属邀请和展示排行榜。国家／地区由你选择，不是精确定位。
  • 麦克风与录音：无需麦克风权限即可观看和分享。首次进入变身视频卡片时申请麦克风权限；允许后，再次进入或重播会自动使用麦克风，无需再次点击录音。权限是否保留由浏览器决定。拒绝后仍可观看和分享，不会反复请求，可主动重试。普通测量仅上传有效评分数值用于全球角色统计，原始录音不上传；同一参与者对同一角色只计一次，公开结果仅含汇总分数与人数。在好友邀请中授权麦克风后，约 6 秒的录音和游戏评分会上传，录音仅邀请人可播放。关联信息包括昵称或访客标签、音高、强度、时长和时间。可在发送前取消，上传失败可重试。游戏分数不代表真实恐惧程度，也不用于身份识别。
  • 浏览器与运行数据：Cookie 维持登录；本地存储保存偏好与本机分数。统计包括页面前台可见时长、角色卡片打开及分享、授权、录音、上传步骤，用于改进流程和排错，不包含额外的设备指纹。服务器及托管服务还处理必要的网络／安全信息，用于运行和防滥用。

登录、分享与可见范围

登录由第三方身份服务（包括 Supabase 及所选 SNS 登录提供方）验证；提供方可能依据你授权的范围处理账号资料。本游戏不索取 SNS 密码，也不导入联系人列表。WhatsApp、Messenger 等分享由你在对应应用中选择接收人并确认；分享平台按自己的政策处理内容。公开邀请链接可被转发，并可能显示邀请者昵称和角色；它不授予查看私密录音的权限。排行榜可显示昵称、所选国家／地区及游戏成绩。

托管、身份验证和存储服务提供方在提供服务所需范围内处理数据，服务器可能位于你所在国家／地区之外。好友录音会按上述方式提供给邀请人；依法要求时也可能披露必要数据。

保存、安全与选择

账号、邀请、游戏记录和上传录音会在提供相应功能所需期间保存。退出登录、清除浏览器数据或移除 SNS 授权不会自动删除服务器数据。可在浏览器设置中撤销麦克风权限；这样可能无法使用需要麦克风的玩法。我们使用 HTTPS、登录会话与访问权限限制来保护数据。

你可以申请查询、更正或删除与自己有关的数据。删除范围、身份核验、备份与必要保留的处理方式，请参阅用户数据删除说明及联系方式。本政策变更会在本页更新日期；涉及新的数据用途时会另行说明。

联系我们

隐私、数据查询、更正或删除申请，请发送邮件至 support@hellotalk.com。请在标题中注明 Halloween Tricks。我们将通过该邮箱回复并核验申请。

Privacy Policy

Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk · Updated October 31, 2026

This policy covers the Halloween Tricks by HelloTalk web game at helloween.fly.dev.

Data and purposes

We process browser guest identifiers, verified account identifiers, your chosen nickname and country/region, progress, scores, invitation links and interactions to operate the game, restore accounts, associate invitations and show rankings. Your country/region is selected by you, not precise location.

Watching and sharing do not require microphone access. Microphone permission is requested on your first transformation card. Once allowed, later visits and replays capture automatically without another recording action. The browser controls permission persistence. Refusal does not block watching or sharing or trigger repeated requests; you can retry. Ordinary measurement uploads valid numeric scores, never raw audio, for aggregate character scores with one contribution per participant and character. In an invitation, granting microphone access automatically uploads about six seconds of audio and the game score for the inviter alone to play. Associated data includes a nickname or guest label, pitch, intensity, duration and time. You can cancel before sending and retry a failed upload. Scores do not measure actual fear and audio is not used to identify you.

Cookies maintain sessions; local storage holds preferences and local scores. Analytics include foreground page duration, character card views, and sharing, permission, recording and upload steps to improve the flow and diagnose problems. We do not collect additional device fingerprints. Necessary network/security information processed by the server and hosting services supports operation and abuse prevention.

Providers and sharing

Supabase and your chosen SNS provider verify sign-in and may process account information within your authorization. We do not request SNS passwords or import contact lists. You select recipients and confirm WhatsApp/Messenger shares in those services, which apply their own policies. Public invitation links can be forwarded and may display the inviter's nickname and character, but do not grant access to private recordings. Rankings may show nicknames, chosen countries/regions and scores.

Hosting, authentication and storage providers process data as needed to deliver the service; processing may occur outside your country. Invitation recordings are shared with the inviter as described above. Necessary disclosures may also be made when required by law.

Retention and your choices

Account data, invitations, game records and uploaded audio are retained as needed for their features. Signing out, clearing browser storage or removing SNS authorization does not automatically erase server data. You can revoke microphone access in browser settings, which may prevent microphone-dependent play. HTTPS, sessions and access controls protect data.

You may request access, correction or deletion. See Data Deletion Instructions and contact details for verification, scope and backup/retention handling. We update the date here when this policy changes and explain new data uses separately.

Contact

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, email support@hellotalk.com and include Halloween Tricks in the subject. We reply and verify requests through this address.
